# AI ARTISAN HUB

AI ARTISAN HUB is an AI-powered artisan marketplace and social commerce platform built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## Project Overview

AI ARTISAN HUB is designed to connect four major user groups:

- Artisans
- Buyers
- Delivery Partners
- Administrators

The platform combines:

- AI-powered assistance
- Artisan marketplace
- Social commerce
- Real-time communication
- Orders and payments
- Delivery management
- Analytics
- Premium animated user interface

## User Roles

### Artisan

Artisans can:

- Create and manage products
- Manage their store
- Manage inventory
- Receive and process orders
- Track earnings
- Create stories and promotional content
- Communicate with customers
- Use AI-powered business assistance

### Buyer

Buyers can:

- Discover products
- Search products
- View product details
- Add products to cart
- Purchase products
- Track orders
- Follow artisans
- Create collections
- Chat with artisans
- Use AI-powered shopping assistance

### Delivery Partner

Delivery partners can:

- View delivery jobs
- Accept delivery tasks
- Manage pickup and delivery
- Track delivery status
- Upload proof of delivery
- View earnings
- Use AI-powered route assistance

### Administrator

Administrators can:

- Manage users
- Verify artisans
- Moderate products and content
- Manage orders
- Monitor delivery operations
- Manage payments and reconciliation
- View analytics
- Monitor platform operations

## AI System
